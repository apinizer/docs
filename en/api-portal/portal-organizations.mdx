**What Is It For?**

* Centrally manages consumer organizations in the portal context
* Shows members, subscriptions, applications, and limits together on the organization page
* Handles risky or duplicate organizations safely via deactivate, merge, and consumer revoke
* Uses verified email domains so signups become join requests to the right organization
* Reduces incorrect manual edits on LDAP-sourced organizations with read-only fields

**Who Uses It?**

* **Portal administrators:** To manage the consumer organization inventory and lifecycle
* **Support / operations:** To see a troubled organization's subscriptions and apps in one place
* **Identity / security teams:** To apply critical actions such as deactivate, revoke, and merge

## Access and Permissions

* In the admin panel follow **Portal → Consumers → Organizations**
* The same list and organization page also open under **Identity → Consumer → Organizations**
* From Portal you work in the active portal context; from Identity you work in the identity-management context. The tabs of the organization page follow the menu that opened it

## List

From the list you search organizations and open the organization page.

Filters: Project, Search by name, accounts, apps, consumer search. Columns include name, source, parent organization, account/app/consumer/sub-org counts, last synchronized, and status (Pending approval, Rejected, or Merged badges when it applies).

Row menu (⋮): **Open** · **Duplicate** · **Delete** · **Move to Global** — for a synchronized (LDAP-sourced) row, Duplicate and Delete are not offered.

## Organization Page

The header shows the organization name, an Active / Deactivated indicator (or a Pending approval / Rejected / Merged badge), and a Synced badge for organizations that come from a directory. The **How to Read** button explains each tab.

### Tabs

| Tab | Content |
|-----|---------|
| **Overview** | Code, names, parent organization, verified e-mail domains, source type, member since, status, and notification mails. **Configure** on a card opens its form; Save first shows what changes and where it applies, then saves and deploys. |
| **Members** | Portal accounts of the organization with their roles. Portal context only. |
| **Consumers/Applications** | The API identities of the organization. Identity context only. |
| **Apps & Subscriptions** | Subscriptions with plan and status (Active, Waiting for approval, Rejected, Disabled, Unsubscribed), and the apps with their registered products and consumer metadata. Secrets are never shown. Portal context only. |
| **Limits** | Limit plans of the organization: the organization total, or a counter for each API client, consumer, or sub-organization under it. Identity context only. |
| **Metadata** | Key-value pairs added to the tokens of its consumers; a consumer's own key wins. |
| **Source** | Manual, or bound to an identity directory. A synchronized organization takes its code, names, and parent from the source. |

**Adding a member on the Members tab:** since a consumer organization is not tied to a single portal, the "add member" picker lists accounts from **every portal** that can reach the organization, not only the active one; accounts already a member of the organization are excluded automatically. Each row also shows the account's portal name so people with the same display name in different portals stay tellable apart.

### Critical operations

Open the **⋯** menu at the top of the page.

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Status > Deactivate / Activate** | Deactivate rejects the requests of every consumer, application, and API client of the organization and of its sub-organizations right away. Nothing is deleted. Activate lets them work again, unless an organization above it is deactivated; a consumer disabled on its own stays disabled. Both show the same dialog first. |
| **Organization > Revoke consumers** | Permanently revokes every consumer of the organization's apps; cannot be undone, and the apps need new consumers. Tokens already issued to the API clients under the organization and its sub-organizations stop working as well. Revoking does not lift a deactivation; the only way to lift it is an explicit **Activate**. |
| **Organization > Merge into…** | Merges into another organization; members, apps, and subscriptions move; quota counters continue; irreversible |
| **Transfer** | **Export** and **Move to Global** |
| **History > Audit and Rollback** | Shows the change history and restores an earlier version |

**Verified e-mail domains:** Portal signups from these domains do not create a new organization — they become a join request. Member approval is done by an Owner on the Developer Portal → [Portal Membership and Invitation](/en/api-portal/portal-membership-and-invitation).

:::warning
Client secrets are **never** shown on this screen. Revoke and Merge are irreversible; confirm impact first. Delete can affect account access / apps / consumers.
:::

## Roles and Statuses

| Role | Meaning (portal membership) |
|------|-----------------------------|
| **Owner** | Member management + write access |
| **Developer** | App / subscription / consumer write |
| **Viewer** | Read-only; does not see client secrets |

Organization status: Active · Pending approval · Rejected · Merged

## Usage Scenarios

* Deactivate a consumer organization whose contract has ended
* Merge two duplicate organizations into one inventory
* Check the subscriptions and apps of an organization that opened a support ticket
* On LDAP-sourced orgs, treat code/name as read-only and use operational actions only
