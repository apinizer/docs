:::warning Deprecated

The operations of this section are deprecated. They still work. Use the [Consumers API](/api-reference/consumers) instead.

Every response carries a `Deprecation: @1792368000` header and a `Link` header with `rel="successor-version"`.

:::
## Endpoints

### JWK Validation and Sign

- [Set JWK Validation](/api-reference/credentials/jwk-settings/set-jwk-validation) - Assign a JWK for validation and signing to a consumer
- [Remove JWK Validation](/api-reference/credentials/jwk-settings/remove-jwk-validation) - Remove JWK for validation and signing from a consumer

### JWK Decryption and Encryption

- [Set JWK Encryption](/api-reference/credentials/jwk-settings/set-jwk-encryption) - Assign a JWK for decryption and encryption to a consumer
- [Remove JWK Encryption](/api-reference/credentials/jwk-settings/remove-jwk-encryption) - Remove JWK for decryption and encryption from a consumer
